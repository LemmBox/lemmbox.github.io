LemmBox - Features

This mod is based off of UltraBox

Meaning it has everything found in UltraBox 2.2.13 feel free to look on the BeepBox wiki for everything that is in UltraBox right here.

Things that make LemmBox diffrent from UltraBox

aka all the stuff I did

  • Tempo slider goes up to 1000BPM
  • Expanded octave range from -8 to +8 octaves
  • High-quality chipwaves:
    • HD Rounded
    • HD Triangle
    • HD Sawtooth
  • A custom chipwave that was made by mistake - "Trapasquare"
  • New theme "LemmBox Dark"

    (Just called "LemmBox" in the theme selector as there is no LemmBox light mode yet

  • Ability to export .txt files
  • Ability to export .png files
